Walk your unit through the memory bay, pick up a block, and carry it to the cache rack. Each mapping policy enforces a different rule for where a block is allowed to land.

What the three policies mean

Direct-Mapped — each block has exactly one legal slot, given by (block address) mod (number of slots). Simple and fast, but two blocks that share a slot keep evicting each other.

Fully Associative — a block can land in any slot. You decide which occupant gets evicted once the rack is full, so you act as the replacement policy.

2-Way Set-Associative — slots are paired into sets. A block maps to one set, by address mod number of sets, but either slot in that set will accept it. A middle ground between the other two schemes.
